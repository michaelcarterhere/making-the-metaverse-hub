  Michael has spent several years advising technology startups, helping founders with product development and go-to-market strategy. He is a professor and program coordinator in the School of Applied Science and Computing at St. Lawrence College, where he also teaches in the Bachelor of Commerce in Social Innovation program.
  His recent ventures include an image generator for content creators and FAQsy, an EdTech firm. He also founded ontheblockchain.ca, hosts the podcast Making the Metaverse, and co-edits When Sparks Speak, an online publication celebrating women leading in tech.
  He studied business at Toronto Metropolitan University before becoming a researcher focused on technology adoption at Queen’s University and the University of Toronto. He also holds an Executive MBA.
