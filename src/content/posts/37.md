# 087 - Dr. Stefania Pecore

Today’s guest is someone who has lived at the intersection of language, learning, and artificial intelligence long before most of the world was paying attention.

Stefania began with a degree in language studies, then moved into machine learning with her first master’s in 2011. She went on to complete a second master’s in natural language processing, a doctorate in artificial intelligence, and a postdoc at the University of Ottawa, finishing just before the release of ChatGPT. She now works with UNESCO as an AI ethics expert, and after her postdoc she launched “Don’t Tech Stress,” a platform that helps non-technical people understand AI and supports founders building AI-powered products.

In this episode, Michael Carter talks with Dr. Stefania Pecore about her journey from a small town in Italy, the challenges of navigating identity as an immigrant, the evolution of AI, its applications in mental health, and the importance of ethics in technology. Stefania also talks about curiosity and collaboration in the tech industry, including supporting women in tech, and about personal growth and the transformative power of AI.
