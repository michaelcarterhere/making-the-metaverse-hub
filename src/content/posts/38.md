# 088 - Jacob Dubin

These days, anyone can build a working app in an afternoon. You describe what you want, an AI writes the code, and a few minutes later something is running on your screen. But a working demo isn't a launch. What happens when someone enters data you didn't expect, or a service you depend on disappears? And when an AI agent acts at machine speed, who actually gave it permission?

Guest Jake Dubin has spent 25 years thinking about systems that can't afford to fail. He built the ticketing industry's first real-time exchange, technology that ended up with StubHub. He's led technology at Ticket Solutions, VeriShip and Tickets For Less. He's run Transcendent Software since 2009, and he's now building CoffeeBreak, starting with Launch Check, a tool that reviews AI-built apps before they go live.

This episode is about what really changes when AI does the building, why failure deserves a design, and where human judgment has to stay in the loop.
